    Enemy pattern

    Aura's next move stays hidden. She reads your last few turns, then chooses. She cannot see the move you play this turn.

    1. Attack4 damage, or 8 after FocusCosts nothing
    2. Guard15 MPHalves damage taken
    3. FocusRestores up to 35 MPNext Attack deals 8
    4. UltimatePrepare for 30 MPCasts next turn for 10

    Under 15 MP, she uses Focus. After Prepare, she casts on the next turn. If your Attack is focused or your Ultimate is ready, she Guards when she has the MP. A string of Attacks draws Guard more often.

    Her Guard halves the hit, Ultimate included. Land the last blow and she does not strike back. Cooldowns advance only on a move that goes through.
