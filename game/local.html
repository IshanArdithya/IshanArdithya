    How to play
    • Attack: 5–7 damage for 10 mana; 14–18 for 20 mana when focused. 10% critical chance (×1.5, rounded down).
    • Guard: 15 mana, take at most 1 damage, and preserve Focus and ultimate preparation. Take one other turn before guarding again.
    • Focus: restore up to 40 mana and empower the next normal Attack. You can refill mana while focused. Focus does not prepare Ultimate.
    • Prepare Ult / Cast Ult: first spend 80 mana to prepare; Aura responds. On a later turn, cast for 32 fixed damage at no further mana cost. Preparation persists through Attack, Guard, and Focus. Casting preserves Focus and starts a six-turn cooldown; no critical hits.

    Frieren: 26 HP / 240 MP. Aura: 100 HP, starting with 40/60 MP. Aura cycles Guard (15 MP) → Attack (4 damage, free) → Charge (+35 MP) → Assault (10 damage, 30 MP). If she cannot afford Guard or Assault, she restores mana instead.

    Aura’s Guard halves every attack, including the ultimate. A killing blow prevents retaliation. Cooldowns tick only when someone plays a valid turn. These numbers and the ultimate form are game adaptations; the full Scales of Obedience contest remains a future feature.
