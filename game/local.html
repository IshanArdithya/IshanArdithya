How to play
  • Attack: deal 5–7 damage, or 14–18 while charged. Each attack has a 10% critical chance for 1.5× damage, rounded down.
  • Guard: take at most 1 damage this turn and keep your charge.
  • Charge: power up your next attack. Charging leaves you exposed, and cannot stack.

Aura repeats Guard → Attack (4) → Charge (0) → Assault (10). Her Guard halves your final damage, rounded up. Her Charge is an opening before a strong assault. A killing blow prevents retaliation.

This is a simplified encounter inspired by her army and scales. Her full obedience magic and Frieren’s extra abilities are reserved for the next phase.
