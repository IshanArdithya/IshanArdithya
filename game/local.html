How to play
  • Attack: 5–7 damage for 10 mana; 14–18 for 20 mana when charged. 10% critical chance (×1.5, rounded down).
  • Guard: 15 mana, take at most 1 damage, and preserve charge. Take one other turn before guarding again.
  • Charge: restore up to 40 mana and prepare an Attack or Ultimate. You can refill mana while already charged.
  • Ultimate — Unleashed Zoltraak: 32 fixed damage for 80 mana. Requires and consumes charge. Six other accepted turns before reuse; no critical hits.

Frieren: 26 HP / 240 MP. Aura: 100 HP, starting with 40/60 MP. Aura cycles Guard (15 MP) → Attack (4 damage, free) → Charge (+35 MP) → Assault (10 damage, 30 MP). If she cannot afford Guard or Assault, she restores mana instead.

Aura’s Guard halves every attack, including the ultimate. A killing blow prevents retaliation. Cooldowns tick only when someone plays a valid turn. These numbers and the ultimate form are game adaptations; the full Scales of Obedience contest remains a future feature.
